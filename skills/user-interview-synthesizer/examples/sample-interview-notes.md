# Raw interview notes: Ledgerly invoicing, payment reminders study

Fictional product and fictional participants, used to demonstrate the
user-interview-synthesizer skill. Ledgerly is a fictional invoicing tool
for freelancers. These are raw notes as an interviewer might paste them
in, unedited.

## P1, freelance graphic designer, 3 years using Ledgerly

Uses Ledgerly for about 15 invoices a month. Says the main pain is chasing
late payments.

"I send the invoice and then I just... forget until the client emails me
asking why I'm bugging them, except I never bugged them. I have no idea
who's overdue unless I open a spreadsheet I keep on the side."

Currently keeps a separate Google Sheet with due dates because Ledgerly
doesn't surface overdue invoices anywhere obvious. Checks it maybe once a
week. Says late payments cost her real money because she has fixed
monthly expenses.

"If there was just a list, like 'these three are overdue,' I would not
need the spreadsheet at all."

Asked about reminders specifically: "I didn't even know Ledgerly could
send reminders until you just said that. Is that a real feature?"

## P2, freelance copywriter, 8 months using Ledgerly

Sends 5 to 8 invoices a month. Says she manually re-sends the invoice PDF
by email when a client is late, usually after client contacts her first
or after she remembers.

"I know I should be more on top of it but I don't have a system. I just
kind of remember, badly."

Mentioned trying the built-in reminder toggle once: "I turned it on for
one invoice and then never checked if it actually sent anything. No idea
if it worked."

When shown the reminder settings screen during the session, did not
notice the "last sent" timestamp field until pointed out. "Oh, that's
useful, I didn't see that at all."

## P3, solo web developer, 1 year using Ledgerly

Sends 20+ invoices a month across several retainer clients. Has a
consistent process: checks a dashboard every Monday.

"Ledgerly's dashboard doesn't sort by due date, only by invoice number, so
I export to CSV every Monday and sort it myself in Excel. Takes maybe ten
minutes."

Says this works for him but is annoyed it requires the export step every
time. Did not mention wanting automated reminders, said he prefers to
message clients personally because "a robot email feels cheap for a
retainer client."

## P4, freelance photographer, 2 years using Ledgerly

Sends 10 to 12 invoices a month. Similar to P1, does not track overdue
invoices systematically.

"Honestly by the time I notice an invoice is late it's been like three
weeks. I should probably have an alert or something."

Asked what would help: "Just tell me when something's late. I don't need
it to be fancy."

Also mentioned, unprompted, that the invoice creation flow itself is slow
because it requires re-entering client details even for repeat clients.
"Every single time I have to retype the client's address. It's the same
five clients."

## Interviewer notes

All four participants were recruited from the Ledgerly beta mailing list,
which skews toward newer users who opted into feedback requests. Session
length was 20 minutes each, conducted over video call, same week.
